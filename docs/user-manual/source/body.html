About this manual

The MHAO Internal Audit Portal is the system the Group Internal Audit team, management and executives use to plan audits, run engagements, issue findings, track corrective actions and exchange control questionnaires. This manual explains every screen, button and email you will meet, in the order you meet them in the app.

Conventions used

  • Bold text is a button, tab or field name exactly as it appears on screen.
  • Italic text is a status value or a message shown by the app.
  • "Who:" lines say which roles can use a screen. A tab or button that is missing for you is hidden on purpose because your role does not allow it.
  • Test mode marks behaviour that applies while the system is being tested (see section 1.4).

1. Before you start

1.1 Opening the app

Open the link you were sent for MHAO Internal Audit Portal (a Power Apps canvas app). Sign in with your MHAO Microsoft 365 account. The first time, Power Apps asks permission to use the app's connections (SharePoint, Outlook, Power Automate). Choose Allow. Use a current version of Edge or Chrome on a computer; the app is laid out for a wide screen.

Your name appears at the top right of the navy header on every main screen. Under it is a small gold reload icon (tooltip Refresh data). 2. Finding your way around

2.1 The navigation bar

A dark navigation bar runs along the top of every screen. It shows only the tabs your role may use, always in this order:

Dashboard  |  Plan  |  Engagements  |  Questionnaires  |  My Questionnaires  |  Fieldwork  |  Findings  |  Actions  |  Follow-Up  |  Reports  |  Resource Centre

The tab for the screen you are on is highlighted. Select a tab to go there. Moving between tabs does not save anything, so save a form before you leave it.

2.2 Common controls

  • Search and filters at the top of a list narrow it (for example by year, entity, rating or status).
  • Select a row to see its detail on the right or below. Most screens have an EDIT or OPEN button for the full form.
  • + NEW (shown only to roles allowed to create) opens a blank form.
  • SAVE / SUBMIT stores your changes. A green banner confirms; a red banner explains what is wrong, usually a required field that is empty.
  • CANCEL or BACK returns to the list without saving.
  • Required fields are marked with a red asterisk. The form will not save until they are filled.
  • Greyed fields are filled in by the system (for example references, status stamps and entity) and cannot be typed into.

2.3 Messages you may see

Message colourMeaning
Green bannerThe action worked (saved, submitted, data refreshed).
Blue / grey bannerInformation, for example Coming soon - document not uploaded yet in the Resource Centre.
Red bannerSomething is missing or not allowed. Read it and correct the field.

2.4 Dates and people

For date fields use the calendar pop-up rather than typing; typed dates may not be accepted. Person fields (Lead Auditor, Owner, Assigned Auditor, Audit Manager) are pickers: start typing a name and choose the person from the list. The list shows display names, not account numbers.

3. Dashboard

Who: Owners, Managers, Auditors, Executives, Department Heads and BU Heads.

The Dashboard is the landing screen for the audit team and executives. It has:

  • Five tiles: open findings, critical open findings, open actions, overdue actions and questionnaires to review. Counts refresh when you select the Refresh icon.
  • Needs attention - overdue actions: actions past their due date and not closed, oldest first. Select a row to open the action.
  • Insights: open findings by rating (Critical, High, Medium, Low), actions due in the next 30 days, and findings closed out of the total.
  • Open Power BI report (top right): opens the Power BI report described in section 11.1, once the report link has been set up by the administrator.

Management roles see only the records they have been granted access to.

4. Annual audit plan (Plan)

Who: Owner and Manager create and edit; Auditors and Executives read.

4.1 Reading the plan

Open Plan and choose the Plan Year (and, if needed, the entity filter). Each row shows the audit reference (IA-year-nnnn), the audit area, entity, assigned auditor, planned dates, budget hours and the approval status. A plan item saved without a Plan Year will not appear under the year filter, so always set the year.

4.2 Creating or editing a plan item

  1. Select + NEW (or select a row and EDIT).
  2. Pick the Audit Area from the Audit Universe. The risk score and rating fill in by themselves.
  3. Choose the Plan Year, Entity, planned start and finish, Assigned Auditor, Audit Manager and Budget Hours.
  4. Select SAVE to keep the item as a Draft (nothing is sent), or SUBMIT FOR APPROVAL (green) to start approval.

The Approval Status card is read-only. The SUBMIT FOR APPROVAL button is visible only on a new item or an item that is Draft or Rejected; it is hidden once the item is awaiting approval or approved.

5. Engagements

Who: Owner and Manager create and edit; Auditors edit engagements assigned to them; Executives read.

5.1 What is created automatically

When a plan item is fully approved the system creates the engagement (reference ENG-year-nnnn), its working-paper and evidence folders in SharePoint (Planning, Walkthroughs, Samples, Testing, Reports, Management Evidence), the standard milestones, and notifies the lead auditor.

5.2 The Engagements screen

Select an engagement to see its scope, team, stage, milestones and a line such as Findings: 3 | Hours: 120 / 160. If actual hours exceed the budget the line adds (OVER BUDGET). From here you can mark a milestone complete, open the working-paper folder in SharePoint, and select EDIT for the full form.

6. Fieldwork

Who: Owner, Manager, Auditor.

Use Fieldwork during an audit to record observations quickly. Choose the Engagement (the information line shows its type, manager and lead auditor), enter the title, observation, root cause and a provisional rating, and save with SAVE AS DRAFT. The observation is stored as a Draft finding that you complete later in Findings. NEW WORK PAPER starts a working-paper record. Draft observations are listed on the right.

7. Findings

Every finding follows the same route: Draft → Manager Review → Management Response → Audit Review → Head of IA approval → Issued → Closed. Finding references look like FND-year-nnnn.

7.1 For auditors

  1. Open Findings, filter by rating, engagement or status, and select + NEW (or open a Draft created in Fieldwork or from a questionnaire).
  2. Choose the Engagement. The Entity fills in from it and is then read-only. The information line shows the audit type, manager and lead auditor.
  3. Complete title, department, observation, root cause, risk, recommendation and Rating (Critical, High, Medium, Low). Save.
  4. Set the stage to Manager Review. The Audit Manager approves or rejects by email and Approvals. A rejection returns the finding to Draft with comments.

7.2 Management response fields

The auditor records management's position on the Finding form after discussing it with management:

FieldValues / use
Management ResponseFree text: management's reply to the finding.
Agreement StatusAgreed or Disagreed.
Management AcceptancePending, Accepted, Partially Accepted, Not Accepted.
Response DateWhen management responded.
Acceptance CommentsReasons for partial or non-acceptance.

7.3 For management (Action Owner, BU Head, Department Head)

  1. You are emailed when a finding needs your response. Open Findings and select it.
  2. Give your response and your agreement, and propose the action owner and due date. The finding moves to Audit Review.
  3. After audit review and final approval by the Head of Internal Audit the finding is Issued; you, your Department Head and your BU Head are notified and receive access to it.

Only Managers and Owners can move a finding to Issued; the system re-checks this. A finding closes automatically when all of its actions have been validated: when the last open action is closed, the finding is closed and its closure date is stamped.

8. Corrective actions (Actions)

Action references look like CAPA-year-nnnn and are assigned automatically.

8.1 Reading the list

Each action shows a traffic light: green (on track or closed), amber (due within 30 days) and red (overdue), plus text such as Due in 12d or Overdue 5d. Use the filters for entity, department and rating, and the show closed toggle. Action Owners see only their own actions.

8.2 Creating an action (Auditor and above)

  1. From a finding select Add action, or select + NEW in Actions.
  2. Choose the Finding. The Entity fills in from it and the information line appears.
  3. Enter the action title and description, choose the Owner (required person picker), the Follow-up Auditor and the Due Date. Save.

8.3 Updating an action (Action Owner)

Open the action, update the completion percentage and notes, and save. The status moves through Open, In Progress, Complete - Awaiting Validation and Closed. Closed is set by the follow-up validation (section 9); do not set it by hand.

8.4 Uploading evidence

  1. Open the action and attach one or more files with the attachment control.
  2. Select UPLOAD ATTACHMENTS AS EVIDENCE. The files are filed in the engagement's Management Evidence folder and the action is marked Evidence uploaded.

Allowed types: pdf, docx, xlsx, pptx, jpg, png, msg, csv, zip. Keep each file under about 20 MB; larger evidence goes directly into the SharePoint evidence folder. The attachment box lists characters that are not allowed in file names (such as # % & * : < > ? / \ | and quotation marks). The app does not rename files for you: rename the file first if it contains one.

8.5 Marking an action complete

Set the status to Complete - Awaiting Validation. This works only if evidence has been uploaded. If there is none, the system puts the action back to In Progress and emails you that evidence is required. With evidence, a follow-up review is created and the follow-up auditor is notified. The action stays Complete - Awaiting Validation until the auditor records an outcome.

8.6 Requesting a due-date extension

  1. Open the action (it must already be saved) and select EXTEND DUE DATE.
  2. Choose a new date later than the current one, enter the reason and select SUBMIT FOR APPROVAL. A message confirms the request.
  3. The Audit Manager approves or rejects. For High and Critical findings the Head of Internal Audit must approve as well.
  4. If approved, the due date changes, the extension count rises by one, reminders restart and you are emailed. If rejected you are emailed the reason.

8.7 Reminders and escalation

A daily check (07:00 Dubai time) emails the action owner 30 days and 15 days before the due date. A late action becomes Overdue and is escalated to the Department Head; at 30 or more days late it is escalated to the CEO. The Head of Internal Audit receives a daily digest whenever escalations occur.

9. Follow-up reviews

Who: Auditors and above work them; Executives, BU Heads and Department Heads read.

A follow-up review is created automatically when an action is marked Complete - Awaiting Validation. Until the auditor records an outcome it is shown as Open (amber chip) and the detail says Outcome - Open (awaiting auditor testing). The counter Actions awaiting validation at the top shows how many reviews are still open.

  1. Open Follow-Up. Your pending reviews are listed; Managers see all.
  2. Select a review and EDIT. Check the action's evidence and enter your testing notes.
  3. Choose the Outcome: Implemented, Partially Implemented, Not Implemented (a note is required to re-open an action).
OutcomeResult
ImplementedThe action is closed. If all the finding's actions are closed, the finding closes too.
Partially ImplementedThe action returns to In Progress and the owner is emailed.
Not Implemented / Re-openedThe action is reopened and the Department Head is notified.

While the outcome is still blank nothing changes on the action; the action only moves when an outcome is chosen.

10. Control questionnaires

Questionnaires let Internal Audit send a control self-assessment to a department, collect answers by section, review them and turn exceptions into draft findings. Each questionnaire has a reference QST-year-nnnn. Answers are Yes or No, with an explanation and evidence where required.

10.1 Creating a questionnaire (Auditor and above)

Option A - upload an Excel questionnaire.

  1. Open Questionnaires. Select DOWNLOAD TEMPLATE to get the MHAO Excel template and complete it (questions are on the tblQuestions table).
  2. Select + UPLOAD QUESTIONNAIRE. Enter the Upload Title (required), choose the Engagement, and use Attach file for the completed Excel file.
  3. Select UPLOAD QUESTIONNAIRE. A message confirms the upload started; sections and questions are created automatically and you are emailed when processing ends. If the file is invalid you receive an email explaining the problem. BACK returns without uploading.

Option B - create the questionnaire and its sections manually from the Questionnaires screen.

10.2 Assigning and issuing

  1. In Questionnaires select OPEN → on the questionnaire. Sections and their questions are listed on the left; the status (Draft, Issued, Returned, Accepted) is at the top right; ← BACK returns to the list.
  2. Select a section. Under Assign selected section enter the AUDITEE EMAIL (MHAO) and the DUE DATE, then SAVE ASSIGNMENT. Repeat for each section.
  3. When every section is assigned select ISSUE TO AUDITEES. The assignees are emailed and the questionnaire appears in their My Questionnaires.

In the section rows the question text has the widest column; the evidence rule, the status pill and the OPEN button are on the far right.

10.3 Answering a questionnaire (assignees)

  1. Open My Questionnaires. Your sections are listed with due date and status.
  2. Select a section, then OPEN SELECTED.
  3. Answer each question Yes or No, add explanations and attach evidence where asked. SAVE FOR LATER keeps your progress; SUBMIT SECTION locks the section and notifies the auditor.
  4. If the auditor returns an answer, correct it using their comment and submit again.

10.4 Reviewing (Auditor and above)

  1. Open the questionnaire and select OPEN REVIEW →.
  2. For each answer select ACCEPT, or type a comment and select RETURN (a comment is required). EVIDENCE requests evidence.
  3. Select RAISE FINDING where the control is not working. A pre-filled Draft finding is created for you to complete under Findings.
  4. When some answers are returned select RETURN TO AUDITEE (one email is sent). When every answer is accepted select ACCEPT QUESTIONNAIRE. Once accepted the questionnaire is complete; there is no separate Close step.

11. Reports and Power BI

The Reports screen shows summary cards for the annual report, the quarterly report, outstanding findings and audit plan status, with EXPORT COMMITTEE PACK and EXPORT FINDINGS buttons. Executives, BU Heads and Department Heads see issued reports only; Auditors, Managers and Owners also see drafts. The Generate buttons marked needs report flow are not active: reports are produced from the Power BI report instead.

11.1 Power BI dashboard and report

The MHAO Internal Audit Power BI report gives management a visual view of the same data the app holds. It reads the SharePoint lists directly and has three pages:

  • Overview: KPI cards (Total Audits, Findings Identified, Resolved Findings, Open Findings, Compliance Rate, Satisfactory Engagements, Average Audit Duration), findings trend, findings by category, entity, risk level, department and compliance status, top key risks, and audit status by engagement type.
  • Findings and Actions: overdue actions, average age of open findings, due-date extensions, action completion %, validation backlog, late milestones, actual vs budget hours, the findings register, corrective actions and questionnaire status.
  • Reports: a print-ready Internal Audit Report page.

Filters. The Year, Entity, Audit Type and Status filters at the top apply to every page; use the left sidebar to move between pages.

Generating a report. Set the filters, select Download Report (this opens the Reports page), then Export > PDF in the Power BI service (or File > Export > PDF in Power BI Desktop). Send the PDF to the Audit Committee or management.

Opening the report from the portal. Use Open Power BI report on the Dashboard. Until the report is published to the MHAO Power BI workspace and its link added to the app, the button only shows a message that the link is not available yet.

Data refresh. The report shows data as of its last refresh. Blank cards or empty tables mean there is no data yet for that measure.

14. How the Audit Universe risk rating works

Once a month the system recalculates each audit area. The area's risk score is the highest inherent risk among its active risks. The rating is Critical at 15 or more, High at 10 or more, Medium at 5 or more and Low below 5. The next audit date is set from the audit frequency: Annual (12 months), 18 Months, 2 Years (24 months), otherwise 36 months.

15. Administration (Head of Internal Audit and site owners)

15.1 Giving a new user access

A person needs three things before they can use the portal:

  1. SharePoint site. Open the Internal Audit site, select Settings (gear) > Site permissions > Share site, and add the person as Owner (full access, for a tester) or Member (normal use).
  2. Power Apps portal. In make.powerapps.com open Apps > MHAO Internal Audit Portal > Share and add the person. Tick Co-owner only if they must edit the app.
  3. Role in the portal. Open the SharePoint list IA Configuration, find the item for the role (Role.Owner, Role.Manager, Role.Auditor, Role.ActionOwner, Role.Executive, Role.BUHead or Role.DepartmentHead) and add the person's work email to its ConfigValue field. Emails are separated by semicolons with a semicolon at both ends, for example ;a@mhao.ae;b@mhao.ae;. The app reads the list when it starts, so the person must close and reopen the app.

15.2 Good to know

  • Permissions are role-based. What each role may see and do is fixed in the app. You control a person's access by choosing which role or roles they hold. To change what a role itself may do, the app must be edited in Power Apps Studio.
  • Power BI: the person needs a Power BI Pro licence and access to the workspace once the report is published.
  • Flows: Power Automate flows run when the person uses the app. Add people as flow co-owners only if they must edit the flows.
  • Stage and Overall Opinion lists on the engagement form are typed into the app. If a new choice is added in SharePoint, the two lists in the app must be edited too.

15.3 Maintaining the Resource Centre

  1. Upload or replace the document in the SharePoint library Knowledge Documents, in the folder for its group (Governance, Methodology, System and templates, Reference).
  2. Open the list Knowledge Index and find the document's row (Title, DocGroup, Description, DocLink, SortOrder, Version, ReviewDate).
  3. Paste the document's link into DocLink and save. The tile turns navy and opens the document. Clear DocLink to show Coming soon again.
  4. Use SortOrder to change the order inside a group, Version and ReviewDate to track updates. New documents are added as new rows. Press the Refresh icon in the app, or reopen it, to see the change.

Library permissions: IA_Owners Full Control, IA_Managers Edit, IA_Auditors Read.

15.4 Publishing app changes

Changes saved in Power Apps Studio are not visible to users until the app is published. Test on the published app, not only in Studio preview.
